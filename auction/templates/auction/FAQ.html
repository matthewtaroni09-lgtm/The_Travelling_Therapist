When a healthcare facility posts an opening, clinicians and practitioners can bid on that position, representing their offer to complete the entire work term listed.

Clinicians may continue to make offers their services for lower and lower prices (This is your bid!) if they are willing to complete the term for that price. This process continues until the auction timer runs out, at which time, The Traveling Therapist matches the lowest bidder to the facility.

If there is a bid placed in the last 60s before an auction closes, another 60s is added to account for differences in network speeds, and those monitoring the posting.

Healthcare facilities post ads for free. Clinicians bid for free.

The Traveling Therapist charges the healthcare facility 5% of the winning bidder's offer (aka the lowest bidder) at the end of the auction. We charge a maximum of $5,000+HST/tax per transaction for large auctions and a $300+HST/tax minimum.

  1. The Traveling Therapist will connect the lowest bidder and the healthcare facility via email
  2. The healthcare facility and clinician confirm necessary startup documents (insurance, agreed upon method of payment) directly once connected by The Traveling Therapist
  3. Share your auction and healthcare facility meeting experience with The Traveling Therapist so we can share with others in your shoes!

Past auctions are available for all to see, forever, regardless of price.

The clinician has 48h from the time stamped on our email to respond to the healthcare facility via email and confirm their intention to complete the work term. If the clinician does not respond within 48h of a match, The Traveling Therapist engages in the following process:

  1. The Traveling Therapist contacts the healthcare facility as soon as reasonably possible after this 48h period to advise them that the clinician has backed out of the match
  2. The Traveling Therapist gives the healthcare facility 2 options to choose from for a next step.
    1. The healthcare facility may be refunded their payment and the entire auction is reposted on The Traveling Therapist. The fee is then charged at the end of this new auction per usual.
    2. The healthcare facility may choose to accept a match with the next lowest bidder and have their card invoiced for the difference (if any) to account for the new awarded auction price.

By using The Traveling Therapist, you understand that you may not be getting your ideal candidate, but an applicable clinician to to fill your position.

While you're welcome to fill out the comments box when posting an auction stating you'd like the clinician to be rostered for a given skill or have a certain type of experience, we allow any clinician to bid on any auction within their professional scope of practice.

  • Auctions are setup for convenience and ease of understanding for all users of The Traveling Therapist with the below parameters in place. Recall that bids represent the total value the clinician is willing to accept to complete the temporary job as posted by the healthcare facility.
  • Clinicians may only bid whole dollar values
  • The maximum value for any bid is $100,000
  • From the 2nd bid onward, bids are subject to incrementation based on the value of the current lowest bid as listed in the auction highlights bar
  • Bids above $25,000 can only be lowered in increments of $500
  • Bids between 10,000 and 25,000 can only be lowered in increments of $250
  • Bids below $10,000 and can only be lowered in increments of $250
  • The Traveling Therapist does not offer automatic/proxy bidding
  • Bids placed in the final minute of an auction add 60s to the timer and end of the auction to be fair to all clinicians interested

  • The reserve price is an optional field available to healthcare facilities when creating a posting.
  • The reserve price is the maximum amount a healthcare facility is willing to pay a clinician to cover the temporary posted that has been listed.
  • If a reserve price is not met by the posting end date, the posting is closed, and NO winner/match is assigned. All clinicians with offers and the healthcare facility that posted the position are notified by email indicating that the reserve price was not met.
  • In our experience, auctions without reserve prices generally tend to attract more bidders, but we leave the decision to or not to set a reserve price up to the facility creating the posting.

Before any auction can go live on our site, our team reviews the ad/posting contents and connects with you as the healthcare facility owner to collect a payment method which we use to pay for your auction upon matching with the lowest bidder.

This serves the dual function of ensuring all our ads are for actual positions available and ensures that clinicians can only bid on auctions that meet their scope of practice.

Approval generally takes 24-48h from the time you created the auction and you will receive an email from us when your auction is live!

Healthcare facilities are invoiced $25+HST per day the contract is set to cover for, inclusive of the start and end date. This means if the coverage is for 8 weeks, the invoice would be $1,400+HST (25/day *56 days). Fee split auctions have the same floor and ceiling prices as fixed fee auctions at $300+HST and $5,000+HST respectively. This means any fee split posting shorter than 12 days of coverage are still invoiced $300+HST and those longer than 200 days are invoiced $5,000+HST.

Please note that the number of days of coverage does not necessarily equal the number of days you have your healthcare worker set to work for. In the above example, the coverage and invoice total is 56 days. Irrespective of how many of those days you have your worker set to work for.

The process for using The Traveling Therapist is much the same as it would be for a regulated Healthcare worker. A consideration we have come across for some using our service is the fact that you might work as both a PTA and a PSW. If this is the case, you will need to create a separate account for each user type when using The Traveling Therapist. This is because a PTA cannot bid on a PSW posting, and vice versa.

Please ensure you read the Terms & Conditions so you understand your responsibilities in advance of using our service. This includes ensuring you are register as the correct user type when you sign up to ensure you can only bid on auctions relevant to you.
