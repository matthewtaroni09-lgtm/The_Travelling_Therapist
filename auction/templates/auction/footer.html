The Traveling Therapist

The Traveling Therapist is an auction based temporary work staffing solution for healthcare professionals. The Traveling Therapist connects clinics, hospitals, retirement homes and long term care facilities looking for temporary coverage for their healthcare workers on vacation, or who are otherwise absent to those providers looking for temporary, casual or extra work in new clinical environments.
