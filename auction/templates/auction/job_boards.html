Hiring Healthcare Staff Is Broken

Healthcare facilities don’t struggle because they can’t post jobs . They struggle because job boards were never designed for urgent, short-term, clinical coverage & don’t offer enough incentive to keep them long term. When a physio is off sick, a therapist goes on leave, or patient demand spikes unexpectedly, posting a job and waiting weeks is not a solution,it’s a liability.

The Reality Facilities Are Facing

Physiotherapy and allied health roles grew 4.9% from 2023–2024 - faster than the national average. Demand is rising, but hiring systems haven’t evolved. Facilities are left choosing between:

  • arrow_forward Paying recurring fees just to stay visible
  • arrow_forward Sorting through unqualified applicants
  • arrow_forward Letting postings sit unfilled for months
  • arrow_forward Overworking existing staff
