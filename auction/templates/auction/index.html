What is The Traveling Therapist?

The best parts of job boards, agencies, and direct hiring—none of the headaches.

The Traveling Therapist connects healthcare facilities and clinicians for short-term contract work. We combine the speed of a job board, the reliability of an agency, and the control of direct hiring—without the middlemen, long waits, or high fees. Facilities keep care covered; clinicians earn more while gaining new experience; patients get uninterrupted service.
