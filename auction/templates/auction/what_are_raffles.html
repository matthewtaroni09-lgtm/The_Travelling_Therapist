Your Activity = Your Advantage

Our new raffle system is a simple rewards game where you can earn tickets through platform actions. Each action earns you a certain amount of tickets.

Challenges:

Sign up for the platform = 5 tickets

Complete a survey = 3 tickets/submission

Refer a colleague = 10 tickets/successful referral

Create a listing = 20 tickets

Place offers on listings = 15 tickets

Your ticket balance is stored on your profile and your tickets never expire, however raffles do, so make sure to spend your tickets on raffles you want before it’s too late! Click on the raffle you want to spend your tickets on, find out more, and choose the amount you want to spend!
