Your Activity = Your Advantage

Our new raffle system is a simple rewards game where you can earn tickets through platform actions.

Each action earns you a certain amount of tickets.

There are challenges that every one of our users can do! However, some are specific for each type.

Clinician Challenges:

Sign up for the platform — 5 tickets (one‑time).

Sign in to the platform once a week — 1 ticket per sign-in.

Complete a survey — 3 tickets per submission (unlimited).

Refer a colleague who signs up — 10 tickets per successful referral.

Place offers on listings - 5 tickets

Healthcare Facility Challenges:

Sign up — 5 tickets (one‑time).

Sign in to the platform once a week — 1 ticket per sign-in.

Complete a survey — 3 tickets per submission (unlimited).

Refer another facility — 10 tickets per successful referral.

Create a job listing — 5 tickets.

Your ticket balance is stored on your profile and your tickets never expire, however raffles do, so make sure to spend your tickets on raffles you want before it’s too late! Click on the raffle you want to spend your tickets on, find out more, and choose the amount you want to spend!

Ticket Earning Clarification

All users have actions that can earn them tickets. However, clinicians and clinic owners have different actions, but they earn the same amount of tickets even though they are different actions.
For example, Clinicians are able to place offers on listing, but clinic owners are not, and clinic owners can create listings but clinicians cannot. However, the different actions are worth the same amount of tickets, so neither type of user has an advantage over the other.
