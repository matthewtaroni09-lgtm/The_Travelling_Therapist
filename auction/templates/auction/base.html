Who is The Traveling Therapist?

TTT is a website service that connects therapists, the bidder, seeking short term contracts with therapy clinics (the “Clinic”), the seller, who are seeking to temporarily fill their staff compliment in order to maintain optimal staffing and patient care, (the “Services”). TTT acts as a meeting place for the Licensed Therapist and the Clinic, therefore the actual contract is between the Clinic and the Licensed Therapist who wins the bid. The therapist utilizing this Site must be licensed to practice in the jurisdiction where the placement is available (“Licensed Therapist”). A Licensed Therapist is defined as one who is licensed with the relevant governing body responsible for licensing such a therapist and such therapist is in good standing with the said governing body, hereby referred to as the “College”.

TTT uses a bidding system, whereby a Clinic in the business of providing therapeutic services, including but not limited to physiotherapy and massage therapy would advertise on the Site, the need for a Licensed Therapist. The Clinic may choose, at its own discretion, to set a maximum reserve price that it is willing to pay the successful Licensed Therapist. A Licensed Therapist will provide his/her bid based on the price he or she is prepared to accept the temporary placement for. The successful bidder is the Licensed Therapist with the lowest price bid that is accepted by the Clinic.
