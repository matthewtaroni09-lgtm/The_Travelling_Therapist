What's The Traveling Therapist?

The Traveling Therapist is an online matching platform that connects healthcare facilities with qualified clinicians for short-term work and provides the opportunity to transition into long-term employment if both parties choose to continue the partnership.

How It Works and Why It Helps

Create a profile and post a listing in under 10 minutes; listings stay live for 14 days while clinicians place an offer on the work, so you get fast, transparent matches without hidden fees. Our pricing model starts affordably and you only pay when we deliver a match, helping facilities reduce hiring overhead while clinicians gain flexible, higher‑value opportunities.
