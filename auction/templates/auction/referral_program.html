What It Is

We reward professionals that help grow our network. For every healthcare worker you refer that completes sign-up, your account receives 10 tickets to be spent on any raffles. Tickets stack — refer more, earn more.

Why it matters

  • ✓ Simple, tracked, and automatic — we handle verification and delivery of the tickets.
