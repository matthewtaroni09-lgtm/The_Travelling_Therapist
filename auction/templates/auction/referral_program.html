What It Is

We reward facilities that help grow our network. For every facility you refer that completes sign-up, your facility receives 10% off your next invoice. Discounts stack — refer more, save more.

Why it matters

  • ✓ Lower operating costs for facilities that regularly employ healthcare staff - whether short term or long term.
  • ✓ Faster matches as more facilities and clinicians join our service!
  • ✓ Simple, tracked, and automatic — we handle verification and delivery of the discount code.

How It Works

Step-by-step

1

2

3

4

5

You receive a 10% discount code applied to your next invoice; we’ll email you the code and show it in your account.
