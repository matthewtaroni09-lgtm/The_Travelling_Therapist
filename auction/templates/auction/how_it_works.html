Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Check out our full FAQ page

You have complete flexibility over your listing cycle. You can manage your matches through three clear paths:

  • Select a Candidate Anytime: Review offers from local clinicians, pick the right fit whenever you're ready, and we will connect you!
  • Re-Post for Free (0 Offers): If your listing receives no offers during its 14-day window, you can re-post it to the marketplace completely free of charge.
  • Accept No Candidate / Clear Listing: If none of the candidates are the right fit, you can select "Accept No Candidate." We invoice you a $50 administrative fee to close/clear the listing, allowing you to instantly re-create or re-post a new listing to capture fresh offers.

A: To protect our clinician community's privacy and time, full clinician name and email is shared with your facility once a match or administrative step is finalized on the platform.

To ensure our clinician community remains active and engaged, The Traveling Therapist requires that listings with active offers result in a hire or pay a standard fee. This prevents misuse of the system and clinicians reviewing jobs and making offers to facilities with no intention of hiring.

If your listing receives valid clinician offers and you choose not to select a match (or allow the 14-day listing window to expire without hiring), a $50 Abandoned Listing/No-selection Fee will be applied to your account.

Clinician contact information is NOT given to the clinic until after they pay the invoice. The clinic may pay and receive clinician info, and decide not to proceed with the hiring said clinician.

If your operational plans change and you must back out of a listing/choose not to hire ANY of the clinicians from your offer pool within the 14 days:

  • A $50 Abandoned Listing/No-selection Fee will be applied to your account to cover administrative costs.
  • Your facility's reliability on the platform may be impacted if cancellations become frequent.

If a clinician cancels a confirmed match, you pay $0 in platform fees.

You can immediately go back to your listing pool so you can instantly select from the remaining clinicians who offered before you selected the previous one. If no backup offers are available, you can re-post your listing on the marketplace.

Your time, value, and schedule availability are protected on The Traveling Therapist. If a clinic backs out of a match:

  • You may start looking for other listings immediately on The Traveling Therapist platform to help you secure an alternative placement.
  • The facility is immediately flagged and reviewed by our team to ensure our platform remains a high-trust environment for both clinicians and clinics.

A: There is no financial cost to you as a clinician for backing out, however your credibility may be impacted on our platform for future listings!
