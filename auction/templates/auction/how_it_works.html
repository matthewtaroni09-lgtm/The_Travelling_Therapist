How It Works

Find a quality candidate in 3 Easy Steps

STEP 1: Post an Ad

Healthcare facilities post an auction using the Traveling Therapist's easy to use auction creation tool.

STEP 2: Bid

Healthcare workers bid with the price they are willing to complete the contract for

STEP 3: Match

Lowest bidder is matched with the facility

  • Healthcare facilities receive an email confirming a match has been made and provide credit card for processing or direct payment via bank/e-transfer. once payment is received, we release the details of your match to you!
  • Healthcare workers, we will also contact you if you were bidding on the posting to let you know if you were the lowest bidder or not. If you are, hang tight, once we receive payment from the facility, we will share your information with the contact person at the facility!

Free listings for healthcare facilities, free bidding for Healthcare Providers!
